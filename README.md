# Skill Composition for Legged Robots

Project page for our research on skill composition for legged robot reinforcement learning, carried out at Sapienza University of Rome.

Specialized skills are quick to train and easy to validate, but composing them reliably is still an open problem. These pages present the problem and our two lines of work on it:

- Bridging: sequential composition, handing control from one frozen expert to the next at any moment
- Blending: concurrent composition, merging the actions of several experts into a single command

The site also hosts the videos referenced in our papers, under static/videos.

Paper: Skill Composition for Legged Robot Reinforcement Learning, AIRO 2026 @ AIxIA 2026.
